The Straight Line is Godless

Hi everyone! Here is my blog. I started it primarily to remember my experiences while studying abroad in the South Pacific, and practice my written voice (although many of you have surely heard this voice for years). It is now serving as a place to store my writings; my thoughts, experiences, and the selection of adventures I want to share with you all, and most importantly: have these writings at easy reference for future Rebekah. The website is purposefully minimal, I keep it to practice html coding in small but consistent ways, but also as a choice to try and reclaim the autonomy that seemingly used to exist in the early days of the internet. No notifications, you simply have to remember to check-- but no problem if you only read this once-- I apprecaite your mind and your time. In the meantime, I hope the clouds are varied and moving in your skies.
