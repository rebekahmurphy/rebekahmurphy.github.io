The Straight Line is Godless

Rebekah's blog, but you know who I am, no matter when you met me. I started this to primarily to remember my experiences while studying abroad in the South Pacific, and practice my written voice, although many of you have heard it for years. It is now serving as a place to store my writings; my thoughts, experiences, and adventures that I want to share with you all. The website is purposefully minimal, I keep it to practice html coding in a tangeable way, but also as a choice to try and reclaim the autonomy that seemingly used to exist in the early days of the internet. No notifications, you simply have to remember to check-- but no problem if you only read this once-- I apprecaite your mind and your time.
Sidenote, but important: Nov 4, 2018, Maggie Rogers performed Fallingwater on SNL. I love this performance, not only for her powerful voice, but also in the way that she seems to be so lost in her music that she forgets she is on stage.
