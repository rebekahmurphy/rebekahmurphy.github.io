When I was little, before every bedtime story my dad would say 'Esta aventura empieza en casa porque todas aventuras empiezan en casa', so in that spirit, here is a photo of my bedroom desk from high school, where I would dream desperately to leave, and be out in the world. Without the longing of adventure from that desk, the reading and the imagining, I wouldn't be able to wander the streets and pathways across (or in) oceans today.
