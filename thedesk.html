When I was little, before every bedtime story, which was always an adventure my dad would make up, he would say 'Esta aventura empieza en casa porque todas aventuras empiezan en casa'.

So in that spirit, here is a photo of my bedroom desk from high school, where I would dream of leaving and being out in the world. It is also the same room where my dad would create the dreams of the adventures, from as early as I can remember. Without the longing of adventure from that desk, the reading and the imagining, I wouldn't be inspired to keep wandering the streets and pathways across oceans today.

It also felt better to have something I feel represents my personality and brain, and rather than have a rotating photo of where I am. The photo represents to me of where “it” all stems from. I often wrestle with what it means for me to be grounded while also being drawn to the clouds. I feel like this photo allows for the anchoring and soaring.

And plus, I really love my room.
