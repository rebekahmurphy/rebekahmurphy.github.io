Curious Fonts

Hello friends and family,

As you may have noticed I’ve been doing a bit of upkeep on the main page for this blog. I tried to upload a photo but unfortunately the wifi does not seem to support this. I hope to continue doing changes throughout the time with the blog as a way to practice and as a form of entertainment. Something I have been pondering is the beloved font for these words. Initially, I did not want to use Georgia because I believed the blog looked too much like Claude's formatting. But now I've decided to embed a poll, mostly just for practice, but also because small details such as this matter! And since I believe in the power of the people, I want to hear from you and what would be most aesthetically pleasing for your eyes. The stakes are very low considering… well… they are all very similar. But that is besides the point and very much not in the spirit of the poll. I look forward to your responses!

Back to regular scheduled programming. This morning we went out snorkeling before class and saw about four reef sharks as well as many beautiful schools of fish. It feels remarkable in itself to see a shark and not find it remarkable anymore. It is still something to note, but I no longer urgently watch it or alert my snorkel buddy. Once we came back we hammered open some dead coral that our professor had brought back for us, outside of the protected zone, so we could see how many living organisms were inside of it. It was really incredible how many organisms were living inside of it, and how diverse they were. Chris spoke about how we only see the “skin of the reef”, and what we were looking at and taking apart was the true diversity that lies inside. Sponges, interestingly, grow inside where the dead coral used to inhabit. The more you know!

After lunch and an afternoon lecture I became so tired and fatigued I tried to nap but it was too hot. I thought to myself that I should just take a shower and get back into bed, but luckily I realized that this would be incredibly dumb since 1) I’m not actually as tired as I feel, it is the heat wearing on me and, 2) when else in my life with the ocean lay out before me to cool me down? I was very grateful I had this self-correction and I had a lovely swim in the lagoon. I realized that once I got out into a particular current, the water was the closest thing I have ever felt to the perfect temperature. The water closest to the shore is too warm, and the temprature past the current I was in is just a bit too cold. I want to say this perfection must be God, because it feels unexplainable, but after this week with Chris and his insistence that the more we learn about ecosystems, the more we realize how nothing is redundant. Due to the awe and provocative statement such as that it makes me think again of the timescale of the world and how it is ever so humbling. It increases my belief that we are simply the most invasive species that the world has seen. Maybe that gives us too much credit, but we sure are some sort of accident, or event like the Ice Age. So maybe what I mean to express isn’t God per say, but something as particular as water temperature surely evokes a feeling of awe.

I have to go get ready for another night snorkel. I have more to say, and based on encouragement from my dad I will be trying my best to write as much as I can.

Sending love and I hope you are experiencing awe in some shape or form,

Rebekah
